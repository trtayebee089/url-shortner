1. FEATURES
2. PRICING
3. RESOURCES

These pages must use the NEW 247URL visual direction established in the previous redesign:

- Light/off-white overall theme
- White surfaces
- Deep charcoal typography
- Indigo primary brand color
- Violet as a restrained secondary accent
- Slate secondary text
- Thin borders
- Very subtle shadows
- 10–14px corner radius
- Modern typography
- Premium SaaS aesthetic
- Clean editorial layouts
- Strong information hierarchy

DO NOT return to the old dark navy/turquoise visual system.

DO NOT simply create three pages made from repeated cards.

These pages should feel like they belong to the same mature SaaS product as the redesigned homepage and dashboard.

The goal is:

FEATURES = explain the product clearly and convincingly

PRICING = make plans easy to understand and compare

RESOURCES = create a useful content/product-education hub

All three pages should look production-ready.
